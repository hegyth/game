# VELRATH - Game Vision

Status: approved product baseline. Statements outside **Open Questions** are established direction. Open questions are not approved mechanics or specifications.

## 1. Game Overview

| Area | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Title | VELRATH |
| Genre | Competitive Multiplayer Action RPG |
| Setting | Grounded Dark Fantasy |
| Platform | Windows PC / Steam |
| Primary format | 3v3 competitive PvP |
| Main mode | Convergence |
| Match duration | 12-15 minutes |
| Camera | Elevated third person |
| Controls | WASD + mouse |
| Performance target | Stable 60 FPS on mass-market hardware |

## 2. Product Vision

VELRATH delivers focused, readable team combat that is easy to enter and difficult to master. Its long-term value comes from mechanical execution, tactical coordination, spatial control, and fair competitive balance without pay-to-win advantages.

## 3. Target Audience

VELRATH is for players seeking short, competitive team matches with direct action controls and meaningful mastery. It should welcome players who are new to the game while retaining those motivated by mechanical improvement, tactical depth, and coordinated 3v3 play.

## 4. Core Gameplay Loop

1. Enter a 3v3 Convergence match as one of the core archetypes.
2. Engage opponents through direct action combat and coordinated positioning.
3. Use Resonance/Echo to influence space through temporary echoes.
4. Pursue the Convergence objective until the match resolves.
5. Apply learned mechanical and tactical knowledge in the next 12-15 minute match.

Exact objective, scoring, and victory rules remain unresolved.

## 5. Core Gameplay Pillars

- **Accessible action:** clear controls and a compact foundational combat vocabulary.
- **Mechanical mastery:** execution, timing, resource management, and counterplay matter.
- **Tactical team play:** positioning, coordination, and spatial control decide outcomes.
- **Competitive integrity:** no pay-to-win power and server-authoritative combat results.
- **Focused scope:** depth within 3v3 PvP rather than breadth across unrelated systems.

## 6. Combat Identity

Combat is third-person, real-time, and built around:

- Light Attack;
- Heavy Attack;
- Block;
- Parry;
- Dodge;
- Stamina.

Combat must support a low entry barrier and a high mechanical and tactical skill ceiling. Exact timings, costs, recovery rules, and interaction priorities require implementation work and playtesting.

## 7. Resonance Identity

Resonance/Echo is VELRATH's defining mechanic. It enables spatial control through temporary echoes and should create opportunities for prediction, positioning, coordination, and counterplay. Its exact actions, limits, duration, and archetype interactions are not yet approved.

## 8. Main Game Mode

**Convergence** is the primary competitive mode:

- 3v3 PvP;
- target duration of 12-15 minutes;
- designed around combat, team coordination, and control of space;
- resolved by server-authoritative rules.

Objective structure, scoring, victory conditions, and tie resolution remain open.

## 9. Visual Direction

- Grounded Dark Fantasy rather than exaggerated high fantasy.
- Elevated third-person camera with clear combat and spatial readability.
- Visual decisions must support competitive clarity and stable 60 FPS on mass-market Windows PC hardware.

Specific environments, characters, palette, and effects language are not yet approved.

## 10. Multiplayer Principles

- The server is the source of truth for consequential combat results.
- Competitive outcomes must not depend on client authority.
- The primary format is balanced 3v3 PvP.
- Multiplayer behavior is considered verified only after testing with multiple game instances.
- Competitive balance must remain free of pay-to-win advantages.

## 11. MVP Scope

The MVP focuses on:

- Windows PC / Steam;
- one primary mode: Convergence;
- 3v3 PvP matches;
- Vanguard, Reaver, and Weaver archetypes;
- the approved foundational combat actions and Stamina;
- a testable, readable Resonance/Echo implementation;
- stable 60 FPS on defined mass-market target hardware.

The MVP should validate combat feel, competitive readability, team play, and the Resonance/Echo identity before expanding scope.

## 12. Explicit Non-Goals

The MVP does not include:

- an open world;
- crafting;
- an economy or trading;
- guilds;
- large-scale PvE;
- a story campaign;
- a large character roster.

These exclusions must not be introduced implicitly by implementation tasks.

## 13. Open Questions

These require design decisions and playtests; they are not approved specifications:

- What are the exact Convergence objective, scoring, victory, and tie-resolution rules?
- Which Resonance/Echo actions, limits, durations, and counters produce readable spatial play?
- What combat timings, Stamina costs, recovery rules, and interaction priorities create fair counterplay?
- What are the final responsibilities and kit boundaries of Vanguard, Reaver, and Weaver?
- Which camera framing and input tuning best support clarity and precision?
- Which hardware specification defines the mass-market 60 FPS target?
- Do playtests consistently support the 12-15 minute match target?
